About

Mathgineering is a software engineering and consulting firm specializing in security, firmware, and cryptography, with deep experience in computer science and mathematics.

Our principal, Christopher Swenson, Ph.D., was a firmware technical lead for the Caliptra open-source platform root of trust at Microsoft, worked on HashiCorp Vault's secrets management and HSM support, and spent many years as a cryptographer for the U.S. Government. He is the author of Modern Cryptanalysis (Wiley, 2008). Previous work also includes Twilio, Google, and several financial startups.

Open-source work can be found on his GitHub profile.
